## Plans

| Plan | Price (USD) | Included |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 1 device, 20 AI actions a month, 30 days of history. A one-time 14-day trial allows up to 3 devices, no card |
| Pro | $5 per month or $50 per year | 2 active devices, then $2 per month for each extra active device. 300 AI actions and 300 cloud-agent minutes a month, 1 year of history, TLS inspection |
| Company | $15 per active seat per month, at least 3 seats | Or $150 per seat per year for a committed number of seats, with extra seats at $15 a month. Unlimited devices, 300 AI actions and 300 minutes per seat, shared. 14-day trial with a card |
| Enterprise and Government | Annual contract | A credit pool and invoices, no card needed. Hub and federation, air-gapped installs, government deployments, TPM attestation, compliance packs |

## Examples

- 2 laptops, normal use: $5 a month.
- 4 devices, all in use: $9 a month.
- A team of 5: $75 a month.
- A team of 2: $45 a month, because Company starts at 3 seats.

## What counts as active?

A device or a seat that is online on 3 or more different days in a calendar month (UTC). Only active ones are billed. Protection stays on for every device either way. A device with no assigned person, such as a build server, is not a seat.

## Credits

Credits pay for AI actions and cloud-agent minutes above what your plan includes. $5 buys 1,000 credits, valid for 12 months. One credit is one AI action or one minute. Any plan can buy credits, Free included.

## What happens at zero credits?

Safety keeps working: blocks, approvals, the secret vault and the audit record never stop. Only AI answers and cloud agents above your allowance pause until you add credits or the next month starts.

## Caps and alerts

Pro starts with a monthly spend cap of $20, which you can change. On Company an admin sets the cap. We email you and show a banner at 50%, 80% and 100% of your allowance and of your cap. Auto top-up buys $10 of credits when your balance falls below $2, at most 3 times a month, and only if you turn it on. The billing page shows your usage and bill so far, with a forecast for the month.

## Tax

Prices are in US dollars, with AED shown for reference. UAE VAT of 5% is added at checkout for buyers in the UAE. Stripe collects tax and your tax number at checkout. Manar is a product of Fixio, which appears on your receipt.

## Trials

The personal trial lasts 14 days, needs no card and unlocks up to 3 devices. After it you move to Free and nothing is charged. The Company trial lasts 14 days and needs a card; it becomes a paid plan unless you cancel, and we remind you on days 7, 12 and 14. You can cancel in one click from the billing page.

## What if a payment fails?

Everything keeps working for 14 days while the payment is retried. After that the workspace moves to Free limits with safety still on. No data is deleted for non-payment within 90 days.

## Can I cancel?

Yes, in the billing portal from Settings. Cancelling stops the next renewal and you keep the paid features until the period ends. Credits already bought stay usable until they expire.

## How do I pay?

By card through Stripe. Companies and government can also pay by invoice and bank transfer. Manar never sees your card details.
