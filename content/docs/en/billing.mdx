## Tax

Prices are in US dollars. UAE VAT of 5% is added for buyers in the UAE. Stripe collects tax and your tax number at checkout. Manar is a product of Fixio, which appears on your receipt.

## Who counts as a billable user?

On the Company plan, anyone who owns an enrolled device or signs in to the console that month. Usage is counted daily and billed for the month. Extra devices are the devices above 3 per billable user.

## What happens when I hit a limit?

You see a message that names the limit and links to Upgrade. Devices already enrolled stay protected. New devices are refused until you are under the limit.

## What if I downgrade, or my trial ends?

- Enrolled devices stay protected.
- Audit older than the new plan allows is hidden, not deleted, for 30 days, and then dropped.
- Cloud agents over the limit are paused, not deleted.
- When a company trial ends without payment, the workspace becomes read-only with export.

## Can I cancel?

Yes, in the billing portal from Settings. Cancelling stops the next renewal and you keep the paid features until the period ends.

## How do I pay?

By card, through Stripe. Manar never sees your card details.
