Manar records what your AI tools did, so you can see it, approve it and prove it. This page lists the data. The [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy) is the legal text.

## What is recorded

| Data | Contents |
|---|---|
| Account | Name, email, the sign-in provider and its identifier |
| Devices | Computer name, operating system, machine identifier, Manar version, the AI tools found and their versions |
| AI activity record | The tool, the action, its target such as a command or a file path with your home folder masked, Manar's decision, your answer and the time |
| Secret alerts | The tool, the process, the type of file, a masked path and the time |
| Billing | Plan, status and Stripe identifiers. Card details stay with Stripe |

By default Manar records what a tool did, not the text of your prompts or the contents of your files. Secret alerts never include the contents of the secret.

## Where it is kept

The hosted service runs on Microsoft Azure in UAE North. Each personal workspace has its own database. Manar's checks run on your computer, and your AI traffic does not pass through our servers.

## How long

| Plan | Searchable audit |
|---|---|
| Free | 30 days |
| Personal Pro | 1 year |

Older records are hidden for 30 days and then dropped. You can export your record at any time.

## Your controls

- **Export** your record from the console at any time.
- **Delete your account** whenever you like. We offer an export first, and the workspace is dropped after a 30-day grace period that you can cancel.
- **Idle free workspaces** with no activity for 60 days are archived and then dropped. We email you a warning before each step.
- **Analytics** on this website are off until you accept them, and run without cookies.

## Who else sees it

Our service providers process data for us under written terms: Microsoft Azure, Cloudflare, Stripe, PostHog (EU) and others listed in the Privacy Policy. We do not sell data or use it for advertising.
