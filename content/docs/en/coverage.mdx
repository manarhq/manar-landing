## How to read it

- **Seen** means Manar finds the tool on your computer and shows it in the AI tools page.
- **Approve or deny** means a request can wait for your answer before the action runs. It needs a hook in the tool or a route through Manar's gateway.
- **Secrets alert** means you are warned when the tool touches a secret file.
- **Secrets blocked** means Manar can stop the read.

## Labels in the console

- **governed:** the tool can be approved or denied.
- **observed-only:** the tool is only seen and alerted on.
- **monitoring unavailable:** the computer is offline or the watcher is down.

## Cells being verified

Some tools may offer hooks that let Manar approve and block directly. We are verifying them tool by tool. Until a cell is confirmed it says Being verified and shows the minimum that holds today. The table here and in the console come from the same source, so they change together.
